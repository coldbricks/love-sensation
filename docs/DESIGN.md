# Love Sensation, after dark

The visual direction comes from the late-1970s nightclub as a theatrical space: black surfaces, reflected silver, warm pools of light, and confident typography.
Love Sensation interprets that atmosphere through an original moon profile and spoon illustration, restrained metallic highlights, a dark interface, and typography with the weight of a printed invitation. The processing controls retain clear labels and generous contrast.

The app icon, repository artwork, and demonstration portrait are original code-drawn graphics. Historical photographs and music recordings are not bundled. The screenshots use fabricated filenames and result records.
