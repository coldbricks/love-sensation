# Love Sensation, after dark

The visual direction comes from the late-1970s nightclub as a theatrical space: black surfaces, reflected silver, warm pools of light, and confident typography.

Studio 54 occupied a former opera house and CBS studio. Its stage infrastructure supported moving scenery and dramatic lighting, including the famous moon-and-spoon set piece. The [Brooklyn Museum exhibition press material](https://d1lfxha3ugu3d4.cloudfront.net/press/docs/Studio_54_Night_Magic_Press_Release_RE.pdf) and [Art Gallery of Ontario exhibition guide](https://ago.ca/sites/default/files/Studio54ExhibitionText.pdf) document those elements.

Love Sensation interprets that atmosphere through an original moon profile and spoon illustration, restrained metallic highlights, a dark interface, and typography with the weight of a printed invitation. The processing controls retain clear labels and generous contrast.

The app icon, repository artwork, and demonstration portrait are original code-drawn graphics. Historical photographs, the venue logo, and music recordings are not bundled. The screenshots use fabricated filenames and result records.
